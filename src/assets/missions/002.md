# SUMMARY:
Over the course of one local night-cycle, Evergreen was subjected to a coordinated, multi-stage Hercynian offensive. Colony security was maintained with significant assistance from contracted pilots. Below is a compiled operational record.

# PHASE 1: PERIMETER BREACH

Status: 
Repelled
Details: 
Hostile units initiated a rapid strike against Evergreen’s southern defenses. Engagement lasted only minutes before enemy forces disengaged in organized withdrawal.
Assessment: 
Attack was a diversion intended to misallocate defensive assets. Union defense team response time and effectiveness prevented deeper penetration.

# PHASE 2: STABLE REACTOR PLANT ASSAULT

Status: 
Partial Compromise
Details:
Stable Reactor 1’s power regulator destroyed prior to PC arrival. Multiple enemy units attempted demolition of Reactor 2.
Outcome:
Regulator preserved due to LANCER intervention until militia reinforcements arrived. Avoided full-colony power failure scenario.


# PHASE 3: ULTRA-CLASS HOSTILE “REGRET”

Status: 

Neutralized

Details:

Enemy deployed a Crisis-era Enkidu chassis designated REGRET, piloted by Anander Danjuro. Hostile breached outer wall and attempted advance on the omninode.

Outcome:

PCs intercepted and disabled REGRET prior to catastrophic self-detonation protocols. Civilian casualties were minimized; structural damage within acceptable thresholds.

Addendum:

Recovered frame components exhibit unique configurations. Further analysis undertaken by LANCER pilots.

# COLONY STATUS:

Primary power: Under Repair

Emergency systems: Online

Hostile forces: In retreat

# RECOMMENDATIONS:

Prioritize restoration of reactor functionality.
Expand perimeter surveillance to counter future stealth approaches.
Continue employing UNION assets for rapid-response operations.
Initiate psychological support protocols for affected civilians.
