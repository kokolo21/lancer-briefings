# SITUATION

Unidentified actors are hijacking Evergreen’s subalterns, drones, and external automated systems. The interference originates from a persistent, encrypted signal broadcasting from the deep forest beyond the colony perimeter. Attempts to remotely isolate or interrupt the signal have failed.

# OBJECTIVE

Union Response Team is to track the signal to its point of origin, gather intelligence on the controlling entity, and disable it, whatever it's source.

# STAKES

If the Union Response Team fails to identify and stop the source of the hostile signal, Evergreen will lose a significant portion of its subalterns, drones, and automated infrastructure. This will severely hinder colony logistics, strain militia defenses, and leave critical sectors - agriculture, construction, medical support - without their primary workforce. Civilian safety and long-term stability will deteriorate rapidly.
